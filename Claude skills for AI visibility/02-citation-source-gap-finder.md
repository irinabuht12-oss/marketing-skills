# 2/ Citation Source Gap Finder

## What it does
AI answers are built from a surprisingly small set of sources — review sites, comparison posts, Reddit threads, docs, Wikipedia. Claude analyzes which sources get cited for your category's questions and tells you which ones are worth pursuing, so you stop guessing where to earn mentions.

## How it works
Paste in AI answers (with citations) from Perplexity, ChatGPT search, and Google AI Overviews for your key queries. Claude extracts every cited URL, clusters them by type (review platform, listicle, community, editorial, docs), counts citation frequency, and scores each cluster by attainability — can you get listed, pitch an update, or publish a competing page that fits the citation pattern.

## Practical example
For "best CRM for agencies," 62% of citations across assistants come from 9 URLs. Claude flags that two are listicles updated quarterly (pitchable), one is a G2 category page (fixable via review volume), and three are Reddit threads (community play). It drafts the pitch email for the listicles and the Reddit engagement guidelines.

## What you get back
- Ranked list of the actual URLs AI cites for your category
- Source-type breakdown with citation share
- Attainability score and recommended tactic per source
- Draft outreach or content briefs for the top opportunities

## When to use it
- After an AI visibility audit shows competitors out-citing you
- Planning next quarter's content and PR priorities
- Deciding whether to invest in review platforms vs owned content
